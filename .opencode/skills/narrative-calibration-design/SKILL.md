## What this skill does

Use this skill for all story text, option text, station investigation text, endings, and narrative pacing.

## Style rules

Text must be:
- short
- cold
- precise
- information-dense
- restrained
- system-like
- slightly painful

Avoid:
- long literary paragraphs
- explaining the whole world
- saying "you were tested" too early
- ghost/horror shock
- vague atmosphere
- text that does not respond to player choice

## Every text line must do at least one thing

It must:
- respond to a player choice
- reveal new information
- upgrade the player's understanding
- imply the system is recording the player
- invite the next action
- mislead or reveal

## Review checklist

After writing text, report:
1. Does it give new information?
2. Does it respond to player choice?
3. Does it change the player's understanding of the system?
4. Does it make the player want to click next?
5. Does it reveal the meta layer too early?
6. Is it too literary or too blunt?
